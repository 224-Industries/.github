## We build in three phases so your site doesn't become the bottleneck

**1. Discover: Guesswork to clarity**
Before any design or development, we work out what the site actually has to do for your go-to-market. You finish this phase with a sitemap, a content plan and a delivery roadmap, all agreed before design starts.

**2. Create: Static site to scalable system**
Design, Webflow build, CMS architecture, animation and integrations. Every component and collection is built so your team can add pages and launch campaigns later without breaking the layout.

**3. Enable: Dependency to ownership**
Once the build is complete we train your team on how to use it: the components, the CMS and the workflows, so pages and campaigns go live on your timeline.

## Why 224?

Because the site we hand over is one your marketing team can actually run. We design and build component-based Webflow sites, so your team can add pages, launch campaigns and update content without needing a developer, and without breaking the layout. You own the site and everything in it.
